Option contract
- Extra gate/damage behavior is enabled only for literal bool use_ocr=True.
- use_ocr=False without force stays OFF. Explicit SELECT_KEEP_OLD,
  SELECT_DROP_OLD,FORCE_KEEP_OLD,FORCE_DROP_OLD keep main behavior; no new
  damage priority runs. This does not mean those modes never perform OCR.
- Public force_ocr=True is forwarded as main FORCE_KEEP_OLD, including main's
  existing override when use_ocr=False. No new normalization/validation added.
- No general old-layer bypass is bundled into a positive-damage feature.
